# Aunt Elsie's Homestay — Content Migration & Conversion Notes

This mockup preserves the useful factual content from the existing public website and reorganizes it around four visitor journeys: students/guests, host families, U.S. schools, and international schools.

## Preserved and reorganized
- Short and long homestay options, including 7, 14 and 21-day stays, seasonal and yearly arrangements.
- Furnished room details: sheets, towels, desk and internet access.
- Continental breakfast and host-served dinner information.
- Airport pickup, drop-off and moving transportation as additional-fee services.
- Cultural adjustment guidance and 24/7 emergency availability.
- Host-family selection and screening language.
- Mission, vision, organizational history, testimonials, school relationships and contact details.
- International-school program planning process and customizable group/program support.

## Removed or avoided
- Unfinished “0+” counters and unsupported statistics.
- Repeated introductory copy and duplicate navigation/hero sections.
- Invented rates, guarantees, response-time promises or service inclusions not verified on the existing website.

## Conversion features implemented
- Audience-specific pages and calls to action.
- Multi-step enquiry form with role-specific information.
- Quote planner and school-program brief builder.
- Clear service-inclusion, matching-process, pricing-factor and FAQ sections.
- Host eligibility and expectations content.
- Persistent mobile call/enquiry actions.
- Page-specific metadata and structured business data.
- Compact premium typography, disciplined spacing and responsive layouts inspired by leading hospitality, education and SaaS interfaces.
