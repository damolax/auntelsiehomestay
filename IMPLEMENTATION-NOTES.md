# Conversion features implemented

## 1. Audience-specific navigation
Students, host families, U.S. schools and international schools now have distinct pages and calls to action. This reduces the time required to find relevant information and prevents one generic enquiry page from serving four different intents.

## 2. Guided three-step enquiry
The enquiry form begins with the visitor's role, then collects placement details, and only then asks for the final message. It stores an unfinished draft in the browser and opens a complete, pre-addressed email to the Aunt Elsie's team.

## 3. Quote and program planners
The website now helps students and schools prepare a usable brief before contacting the team. The planner collects stay length, date, school or destination, group size and optional services without inventing an instant price.

## 4. Transparent pricing guidance
The pricing page explains the factors that affect a custom quote: duration, guest profile, location, room and meals, transportation and program design. It includes the 7-, 14- and 21-day stay options and seasonal, yearly, group and high-school pricing notes from the existing site.

## 5. Service inclusions before the call to action
Furnished room, linens, towels, desk, internet, meal arrangements, transportation options, cultural adjustment support and 24/7 emergency availability are visible before visitors are asked to enquire.

## 6. Trust and screening content
The redesign foregrounds selected and screened host families, practical placement information, direct phone numbers, the office address and the current site's institution and community experience. Unverified counters have been removed.

## 7. School partnership journeys
U.S. schools receive a local housing coordination path. International schools receive the full 10-stage program journey from exploration through future collaboration.

## 8. Questions answered on-page
Role-specific FAQ sections address stay length, meals, commute, airport transfers, host household types, screening and program customization.

## 9. Mobile conversion support
The mobile version includes a persistent Call / Enquire bar, a full-screen navigation menu, large touch targets and responsive forms.

## 10. Search and AI-discovery foundations
The code includes semantic headings, audience-specific page titles and descriptions, Organization/LocalBusiness JSON-LD, visible contact information and clean routes. Production launch should add server-rendered or pre-rendered route content, an XML sitemap, Search Console, analytics and approved FAQ schema.

# Production checks before launch

- Confirm which of the two public phone numbers should be primary.
- Confirm current rates, cancellation terms and payment process.
- Confirm screening language and emergency support procedures.
- Confirm current permission to mention institutions, organizations and testimonials.
- Download, optimize and store approved photographs locally rather than relying permanently on the old WordPress media library.
- Connect the enquiry form to the client's CRM, email platform or a secure server-side form endpoint.
